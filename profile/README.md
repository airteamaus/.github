Airteam is an Australian software design and engineering company. We build software for organisations that can't afford failure, including health insurers, hospitals and national research bodies.

Our team works remotely from across Australia, with everyone onshore. Our processes are certified to ISO 27001 and ISO 9001.

## What we do

- **Design:** validate ideas with real users and settle product decisions before engineering starts.
- **Development:** web platforms, mobile apps and system integrations built around how your organisation operates.
- **Delivery:** governance, deployment and ongoing support that keep software secure and reliable after launch.
